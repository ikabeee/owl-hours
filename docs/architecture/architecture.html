How the pieces talk to each other

Six views of the same system: what the parts are, how each kind of input is understood, what happens when a video arrives, how a job gets confirmed and approved, which states a job moves through, and what the agent can and cannot do. The demo story runs through all of them: Ana's leaking toilet at 12:04 AM, Northside Plumbing, and Mario the technician.

1 / 6

Components

The customer never installs anything; everything reaches them through WhatsApp. The business uses one Expo app for owner and technician. Every model call and every state change goes through our backend.

  • The webhook answers 200 at once and hands the work to the worker. WhatsApp retries slow webhooks, and a video plus three model calls takes several seconds.
  • The business app never calls a model directly. It talks to our API, and it listens to Supabase Realtime for new jobs and status changes.
  • Muse Image only makes ads. Safety instructions use a real video frame with an overlay that our code draws from SAM's box.
  • Blue marks the path of the urgent request, from Ana's video to the models.
2 / 6

Multimodal pipeline

Four kinds of input come in: video, voice, text and photos. Each is prepared differently, and they meet in one place, Muse Spark, as text, a few frames and SAM's detections. What leaves is text, a marked frame, structured JSON or an ad image. There is no spoken output.

  • Blue is the urgent path at 12:04 AM: video and voice in, a marked frame and a safety question out.
  • Two image paths, never mixed. Safety uses a real frame with a circle and arrow drawn by our code at SAM's coordinates. Muse Image only touches the ad, and only after the customer's consent.
  • The customer's voice note is transcribed as a whole file; the owner's hold-to-talk is streamed for a fast answer. Replies go out as text because there is no text-to-speech.
  • Voice Transcribe has no Swahili, so the Nairobi demo should run in English. The puddle signal sorts urgency; it is never shown as an exact number.
3 / 6

From a video to a quote

What happens between 12:04 and 12:05 AM: Ana sends a 14-second video and a voice note, and the owner's phone shows an urgent job with a ready quote.

  • Voice goes through Voice Transcribe first; Muse Spark gets text, frames and the SAM signals, never raw audio.
  • SAM tracks the puddle across frames. "Puddle grows" is a signal for sorting, not a measured number.
  • Muse Spark fills the ticket through tool calls (get_price_rules, create_or_update_ticket). The output is checked against the zod schema before it is saved.
  • The valve frame Ana receives is drawn by our code from SAM's box. No generated image touches a safety instruction.
4 / 6

The owner confirms, the customer approves

Two people have to say yes before Mario drives anywhere. The blue arrows are those two human decisions; everything else prepares or records them.

  • The API, not the model, moves the job to owner_confirmed, and only after the owner's tap. A voice "yes" only answers the assistant's question.
  • Ana's exact address is stored but hidden until customer_approved. Before that the business sees only the distance.
  • The WhatsApp API sends static locations. Check the docs for live location; the fallback is periodic updates or a tracking link.
5 / 6

Job states

Eight states, two human gates. The model can propose any move, but code accepts only the transitions drawn here, and only from the actor named on the arrow.

6 / 6

Inside the agent

Muse Spark sees the evidence and the business's own rules, and acts only through seven tools. Reading is free; writing passes a validator; talking reaches people through WhatsApp or the business app.

  • Read tools return data; they change nothing.
  • propose_transition only asks. The validator checks the state machine and the actor, and rejects the rest.
  • The system prompt keeps the agent on one business's requests. WhatsApp bans general-purpose AI assistants on its business API.
  • ask_owner turns a doubt into a question with buttons in the business app, like the fill valve pickup.
Source of truth for names, numbers and rules: 02-HANDOFF.md. Model IDs and the live-location API still need to be confirmed in the official docs.
